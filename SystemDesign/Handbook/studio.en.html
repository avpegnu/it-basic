1. Use the studio as a design review

Choose a prompt and write requirements, invariants and assumptions before looking at examples. Trace primary reads and writes, including acknowledgements and authoritative state. Add caches or queues only with an explanation of the problem they solve and failures they introduce.

A short practice round can take 10–15 minutes: sketch, inspect arithmetic, walk failures and compare. This is a practice format, not mandatory interview timing. Different designs can be defensible when their assumptions and trade-offs are explicit.

2. Your interactive design workspace

Add components, edit labels/units/capacities and connect From→To with read/write multipliers. Drag nodes or move focused nodes with arrow keys. The shared list editor appears on mobile. Edit edges through their controls; deleting a node removes its attached edges.

Review displays derived rates, ratios and bounded findings. Comparison leaves your draft intact, while loading/resetting an existing design requires a second click. JSON preserves editable data and Markdown preserves reasoning. No external AI service or repository mutation is involved.

3. Prompts and decisions to defend

Short links with hot keys

  • Design creation and redirects at 12000 reads/s and 300 writes/s, addressing expiry, abuse and hot keys. A cache does not replace authoritative mappings.
  • Explain separate read/write paths, cache invalidation, uniqueness and expiry races.

News Feed with fan-out

  • Use 8000 feed reads/s and 400 posts/s, assuming 25 timeline insertions per post. Separate logical requests, internal operations and celebrity exceptions.
  • An edge multiplier of 25 counts operations per post, not user requests. Define deduplication, ordering, ACLs and freshness.

Chat with reconnect and device ACK

  • Use 3000 history reads/s, 2000 messages/s and three online deliveries per message. Connection capacity differs from message-operation throughput.
  • Explain durable acceptance, device cursors/ACKs, duplicate delivery and reconnect recovery. Push diagrams do not prove exactly-once UI.

Checkout with stock and payment intents

  • Use 2500 product/cart reads/s and 200 checkout attempts/s. Assign owners to stock reservations, unknown payment outcomes and webhook replay.
  • Explain authority boundaries and stable business intents; caches and queues do not prove no overselling or single charges.

Upload media and publish derivatives

  • Use 10000 metadata/media reads/s, 100 upload commits/s and four derivative tasks per upload. Model bandwidth and encoding duration separately.
  • Define staging/published manifests, multipart completion and orphan cleanup. CDN hits reduce modeled read operations only.

Search with index freshness and ACLs

  • Use 6000 search reads/s and 800 document updates/s. Indexes are derived state, and permissions can change between retrieval and rendering.
  • Explain CDC/outbox, stale/delete fencing and current authorization. Graph paths do not prove recall or prevent disclosures.

LLM traffic with reservations and streams

  • Use 1500 logical calls/s and deliberately define read/write operation classes. Tokens, streams, first-token latency and provider quotas lie outside the two-rate model.
  • Separate operations, attempts and usage; explain qualified fallback, pending usage and atomic reservations. API operation capacity is not model token throughput.

Your own design prompt

  • Write actors, functional requirements, invariants, service targets and exclusions. Define operation rates and capacity assumptions before review.
  • Name every arrow, its share or fan-out, and missing evidence. Keep trade-offs and failure walkthroughs in notes.

These hypothetical workloads support practice rather than describe real products. Each prompt has a separate editable draft. Custom prompts can cover other case studies; keep tokens, bytes and connection workloads separate when two operation classes are insufficient.

4. Edges express operation multiplicity

Aggregate workload is divided equally among client sources. Edges declare downstream read/write operations per incoming operation. Zero disables a lane, 0.25 forwards a quarter, one forwards all, and 25 produces 25 downstream operations. Branches are not normalized automatically; two ×1 edges express fan-out.

Merges sum arrivals. If paths represent duplicates of the same event, model the deduplication effect deliberately or explain it outside the fixture. The model counts operations rather than proving business causality or stochastic behavior.

5. Cache misses need explicit paths

Cache/CDN nodes multiply forwarded reads by one minus the declared hit ratio, leaving writes unchanged. A leaf cache does not automatically reduce sibling database traffic: draw explicit miss paths or justified cache-aside fractions. Existence does not establish hit rates, invalidation or stampede behavior.

For 1000 lookups/s and 80% hits, an r×1 miss edge yields 200 store reads/s. Two hundred direct writes/s remain a separate lane; r×2 misses yield 400 backend reads/s. This does not predict latency tails, bandwidth or consistency.

For cache-aside, declare justified miss fractions or draw an explicit miss path; avoid applying the same reduction twice. Invalidation, negative hits, retries and shared CPU are outside this model.

6. Define capacity units and SQL write ceilings

Each non-client declares independent read and write ceilings. The model takes the larger load-to-capacity ratio, without shared-resource contention. Positive load at zero capacity yields infinity. The 80% highlight is a teaching convention, not a universal production threshold.

For SQL, units increase the read ceiling while writes retain one total primary ceiling. This illustrates that replicas do not automatically partition writes, rather than excluding sharded or multi-writer SQL designs. Other nodes assume linear scaling, which must be validated for partitioning and skew.

7. Expose cycles and unsupported inference

DAG propagation needs a topological order. A cycle disables numerical rate/utilization inference and displays N/A rather than silently dropping edges. Real feedback and retry loops can be valid but need a different bounded or stochastic model.

You can unfold bounded attempts or declare evidenced amplification while discussing correlation, deadlines and overload. The studio does not model queueing, latency distributions, bandwidth, storage retention or availability, and does not derive p99 by multiplying averages.

8. Findings prompt review rather than prove correctness

Unreachable components, missing source edges, absent modeled store writes and queues without direct workers prompt useful questions. Other topologies can be valid and require explanation rather than automatic rejection. Reaching storage does not establish transactions, authorization or durability.

Warning-free designs can still oversell, disclose data or duplicate charges. Review invariants, acknowledgements, effect identity, authorization and recovery with failure walkthroughs. Arithmetic cannot replace this reasoning.

9. Capacity loss is not an availability calculation

The loss scenario subtracts a declared number of units from each non-client tier. Three units losing one leaves two modeled capacity units; SQL writes become zero only when none remain. This does not choose failed replicas, elect leaders, evaluate quorums or estimate recovery time.

Surviving a zone failure requires placement, dependency, failover, capacity and data-loss evidence. Managed services are neither infinite nor failure-free. Recovery and availability objectives need separate contracts and measurements.

10. Work through arithmetic and failure questions

For the feed prompt, 400 posts/s with 25 insertions each gives 10000 timeline writes/s. Two workers at an assumed 2000 writes/s each provide 4000, a 250% ratio. Five units merely reach 100% under the fixture before retries or skew. Challenge distributions and celebrity handling before only adding replicas.

A worker crash after writing but before acknowledging can replay effects. Explain stable event/recipient identities and cooperating sink writes instead of claiming queue exactly-once behavior. Permission revocation also needs current authorization even on cache/index hits. Capacity ratios do not resolve these issues.

11. Local drafts, bounded import and export

Each prompt keeps nodes, edges, notes, rates and loss scenarios locally. JSON retains editable data and Markdown records assumptions/findings. Drafts do not synchronize across devices and browser storage can be cleared or restricted, so keep exports when needed.

Imports replace the current prompt after version, identity, numeric and edge validation within 100KB/30-node/80-edge limits. Invalid files preserve drafts. Corrupt or externally changed storage pauses saving; export before reloading. Labels and notes remain text rather than executable code.

The 80-edge limit blocks new pairs while allowing edits to existing multipliers without changing edge count. At the limit, remove an unnecessary edge before adding a new pair. Invalid or oversized imports must not silently truncate graphs and report success.

12. Review assumptions and deepen the discussion

Give components responsibilities and state ownership, edges operation units and failure contracts, and invariants authorities. Test peak/skew/loss assumptions alongside retries, partial failure and policy changes. State what was calculated, what remains unproven and what evidence to collect next.

Are two outgoing ×1 edges load balancing?

No. Each receives the full lane. Model load balancing through explicit fractions and distribution assumptions.

Does no warning establish correctness?

No. Structural/load checks do not establish invariants, recovery, security, service levels or actual capacity.

Why do SQL units not increase the modeled write ceiling?

The teaching profile has one write primary. Sharded/multi-writer designs need explicit routing and authority models rather than pretending replicas partition writes.

Can a cycle be a legitimate design?

Yes, but this model does not solve feedback. It switches to structural-only output; use bounded unfolding or a suitable model for feedback.
