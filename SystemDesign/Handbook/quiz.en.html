10. Questions about interpreting quiz results

Does 4/5 among selected answers mean 80% across a ten-question session?

No. Overall correctness is 4/10 while correctness among selected answers is 4/5, with other outcomes retained. State denominators explicitly; neither ratio establishes domain mastery or interview performance.

Should a delayed timer callback allow extra answer time?

Not under this elapsed-deadline contract. Check current elapsed time on selection and resolve expiry first. Callbacks update the display rather than define elapsed time. Pause and platform sleep semantics remain explicit.

Why explain every option even when the key is correct?

Options supply cues and correct choices can be guesses. Rationales expose false premises, scope and unit errors; variations test transfer. An unexplained distractor remains a learning gap despite a correct count.

Should shuffling options change the identity of a selected answer?

No. Keep option identities separate from display order and map digit shortcuts to that order. Grade and export identities rather than confusing source and displayed indices. Retain the session order after selection.
