This Airbnb-style marketplace separates host listings/calendars/rules/prices from guest search and booking. Labs use single-inventory listings; multi-room inventory requires capacity semantics. Stages teach problem-driven design rather than a current deployment inventory.

Search may use bounded, observable projections while booking resolves at authority. Pricing suggestions, estimates, quotes, holds and confirmations are distinct, as are pending, captured, refunded and paid-out money.

2. Define dates, rules and blocking states

Local nights become booking commitments

  1. Search

    Candidates under known coverage/rules.

  2. Quote

    Totals, fees, taxes, currency and consent.

  3. Hold

    Exclusive owned blocking intervals.

  4. Confirm

    Valid ownership and evidence; recover uncertainty.

Retain listing/date/party scope and versions throughout.

Use half-open local-date stays: checkout dates do not block subsequent nights unless turnover rules do. Reject invalid intervals under policy. UTC durations divided by twenty-four hours do not replace local-date counting through DST; hourly inventory is a different domain.

Calendars include host blocks, holds, confirmations, cancellations and external imports. Expiry changes authoritative state before removing blocks. External platforms have independent lag/conflicts; no automatic cross-platform uniqueness exists, so declare risks and policies.

          8. No-overlap requires schemas and authority scope

          Single-inventory exclusion or per-night uniqueness can guard competing writes at a chosen database authority. Reject null/invalid intervals and exclude cancelled/expired states from blocking. Time passing does not itself change row state.

          Time-dependent predicates do not make expiring indexes. Change held state transactionally with guarded versions and intent. Confirmation checks ownership and expiry at serialization, and competing expiry/confirmation has one winner. Stale work cannot resurrect claims.

          Constraints do not solve split-brain, failover loss or independent inventory channels. Define fencing, durability and restore verification. Multi-room capacity needs a different allocation invariant.

          15. Worked questions: allocate time and preserve commitments

          Trace search through confirmation and a failure, separating authority, projections and policy. These answers test assumptions rather than mandate one engine.

          Does a365-bit bitmap solve availability search?

          No. It covers blocked nights in a known horizon, alongside origins, rules, party, pricing, metadata and freshness. Wider operations cost more words and booking still revalidates authority.

          Can active exclusion use WHERE expires_at>now()?

          No. Time-dependent predicates do not implement indexed expiry. Use explicit blocking states and transactional expiry; confirmation validates current expiry/versions despite sweeper lag.

          Does a database constraint prevent double booking across every site?

          Only within its authority/durability scope with valid inputs. Split-brain, loss and independent calendars need explicit coordination/recovery; multi-room capacity differs.

          Can a version guard repair missing CDC events?

          No. Guards reject observed regressions without inventing missing state. Retain capture/barriers and recovery evidence; the fixture floor is a supplied verified barrier, not a free latest oracle.

          Should payment be charged again after confirmation fails?
